Yes. Prepared meals can be planned for individuals, couples, families, seniors, and busy professionals.

Yes. Family meal prep can be customized around household preferences, dietary needs, selective eaters, portion sizes, and busy schedules.

How far in advance should I book weekly meal prep?

Reach out before the week you want service to begin so there is time to plan your menu and account for dietary needs.
