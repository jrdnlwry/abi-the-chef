Common Questions

Private Dinner FAQs

How much does a private chef in Raleigh cost?

Pricing depends on guest count, menu complexity, ingredients, service style, location, and dietary needs. Request a custom quote for your event.

Can I book a personal chef for a dinner party?

Yes. Dinner party menus can be customized around your guests, occasion, and dietary restrictions.

Is this the same as catering?

No. A private chef experience is more personal and customized than traditional catering. The menu is built around your home, guests, and preferences.

Can you cook for guests with dietary restrictions?

Yes. Menus can be customized for gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, vegan, low sodium, allergy-conscious, and other dietary needs.

Do you serve areas outside Raleigh?

Service may be available in nearby communities depending on the date, location, and type of dinner.

How far in advance should I book?

Advance booking is recommended, especially for weekends, holidays, and special occasions.
